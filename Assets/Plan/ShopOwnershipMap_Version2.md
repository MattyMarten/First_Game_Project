## Hire ownership handoff — updated

A survivor at Desk 3 is a Shop-owned hire candidate until the machine process is fully confirmed.

### Spot 1 — Desk 3
The Shop owns:
- hire candidate generation
- stats/trait preview
- accept/reject interaction
- movement to the machine

### Spot 2 — Machine
The Shop still owns:
- temporary hire candidate data
- machine interaction UI
- class/setup choices before confirmation

During this stage, Recruit Quarters may be queried for:
- bed availability
- valid bed choices
- recruit capacity

But Recruit Quarters does not yet own the recruit.

### Spot 3 — Bed confirmation
When the player confirms the machine result and bed assignment:
- Recruit Quarters creates the real recruit
- ownership transfers from Shop to Recruit Quarters
- the recruit is placed/spawned at the chosen bed
